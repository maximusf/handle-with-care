# Handle With Care

A 2D platformer about the laziest delivery job in the hotel business: you're a "Yeet" courier, and the only way to deliver a package is to **kick it**. Get packages into every delivery zone before time runs out, without kicking them to pieces.

Made with Godot 4.7 by **The Muckslinger Gang**: Raven Jaime, Carlos Mendoza and Maximus Fernandez.

## How to play

**Goal:** fill every delivery zone with packages. A zone needs 2 packages sitting in it for 5 seconds; a checkmark means the delivery succeeded.

| Objective | Type |
|---|---|
| Deliver packages to every zone | Required to win |
| Finish in under 5 minutes | Bonus star |
| Don't destroy any package | Bonus star |

- Every kick damages a package. **After 5 kicks it breaks.**
- You **lose** if too many packages break to fill the remaining zones.
- The result screen shows your stars, deliveries, packages lost, kicks and time. **Retry** replays the level; **Back** returns to the title screen.
- Tutorial signs (press **E**) and the hotel guests explain the basics as you go.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D | Move left / right |
| Space | Jump |
| Hold left mouse | Aim and charge a kick (aim with the mouse) |
| Release left mouse | Kick |
| Right mouse (while charging) | Cancel the kick |
| E | Read a sign |
| T | Free camera (scroll wheel to zoom) |
| R | Restart the level |
| Esc | Pause menu |
| F11 | Fullscreen |

Volume, camera zoom and fullscreen can be changed in **Settings** and are remembered between launches.

**Changing the music:** open `scenes/User_Interface.tscn`, select the **User_Interface** node, and drag a track onto **Title Music** or **Level Music** in the Inspector. For seamless looping, select the file, tick **Loop** in the **Import** tab and click **Reimport**. Remember to credit any track you didn't make.

## Running and building

- **Run from source:** open the project folder in Godot 4.7 and press Play.
- **Build the Windows executable:**
  1. In Godot, open **Editor > Manage Export Templates** and click **Download and Install** (one time only).
  2. Open **Project > Export**, choose the **Windows Desktop** preset and click **Export Project**.
  3. The game is written to `builds/windows/HandleWithCare.exe` (a single file with everything embedded).

  From a terminal, after step 1: `godot --headless --export-release "Windows Desktop" builds/windows/HandleWithCare.exe`
